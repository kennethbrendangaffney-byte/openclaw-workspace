# Local LLM & Edge AI Research — 2026-05-18

## 3-Bullet Summary

- **Qwen3.5 & Phi-4 lead 2026 SLM rankings.** Alibaba's Qwen3.5 family (0.8B–7B, Apache 2.0) and Microsoft's Phi-4 are top picks for local/Ollama use, with Gemma 3 and Llama 3.2 1B/3B rounding out the field. Mistral's Ministral 3 8B is a new open entrant targeting efficient edge deployment. All are viable on 8GB RAM or less when quantized.

- **llama.cpp outperforms Ollama by 1.5–1.8x on the same hardware**, per community benchmarks (April 2026). The gap is attributed to more aggressive kernel optimizations and faster GGUF model availability. MoE (Mixture of Experts) model optimizations on Apple Silicon are an active focus in the llama.cpp upstream.

- **Q4_K_M remains the edge sweet spot; Q5 recommended for sub-4B models.** Quantization guides this month confirm Q4_K_M lets a 7B model run on 8GB edge devices. For anything under 4B params, pushing to Q5 is advised — accuracy drops of 5–8% on coding tasks are seen going Q5→Q4. K-quant formats have fully superseded legacy GGML schemes (Q4_0, Q4_1, etc.).

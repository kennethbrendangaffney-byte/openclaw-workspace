# Local LLM & Edge AI Research — 2026-05-19

## Small/Edge Models (1B–8B)
- **2026 is the year of SLMs** — A strong consensus across r/LocalLLaMA and AI community roundups: small language models have reached a quality threshold that makes 1B–8B models genuinely usable for most tasks. Llama 3.2 1B/3B (Meta's compact releases, ~56% smaller via quantisation) and Qwen3 variants are leading the charge in open-weight SLMs.
- **NousResearch finetunes** for 3B, 8B & 30B tiers are active and in active community use, filling gaps left by base model releases.
- **Reddit sentiment** suggests 1B/3B tier is genuinely useful now for edge/IoT use cases, not just novelties.

## Ollama / llama.cpp
- **llama.cpp benchmarks show 1.5–1.8× throughput gains over Ollama** on equivalent hardware (per community benchmarks, April 2026), making it the go-to for performance-sensitive local deployments.
- **Active Ollama GitHub issue** ([#10630](https://github.com/ollama/ollama/issues/10630)) requests update to latest llama.cpp with new MoE (Mixture of Experts) optimisations — particularly relevant for Apple Silicon users. This signals upcoming Ollama improvements are pending upstream llama.cpp merge.
- **GGUF format remains the standard** for sharing quantised models; new models land there first before Ollama native support.

## Quantization Methods
- **SciOpen published a comprehensive LLM quantisation survey** (April 2026) covering the full spectrum: PTQ (post-training quantisation), QLoRA fine-tuning, AWQ, GPTQ, and BitNet-style approaches — good current academic reference.
- **QLoRA remains the dominant fine-tuning + quantisation combo** for running 70B models in 4-bit on consumer hardware. The technique (4-bit NormalFloat + double quantisation + LoRA adapters) is well-documented and production-tested.
- **GGUF / INT8** continues to offer the best safety/compatibility tradeoff for running large models locally without fine-tuning.

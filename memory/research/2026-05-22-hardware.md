# Hardware Research — 2026-05-22

## GPU
- **RX 9070 XT:** Spotted at **$629 all-time low** in an Amazon lightning sale (~4 days ago). AMD continues to undercut RTX 5080 significantly — the 5080 remains roughly 2× the price in most markets outside the US.
- **RTX 5080:** Still expensive. Markups persist above MSRP in most regions.
- **Trend:** AMD winning the value war this generation. RX 9070 XT delivering near-5080 performance at a fraction of the cost.

## DDR5 RAM
- Dramatic price surge over the past year: a **32 GB DDR5-6000 kit that cost ~$80 in mid-2025 reached ~$432 by early 2026** — a 400%+ increase.
- **Good news:** Prices have been **stabilizing and falling** since March 2026, dropping to the lowest levels seen in a while (per HotHardware).
- Current going rate: ~$10–11/GB for decent 32–48 GB kits on PCPartPicker.
- Supply still constrained by AI/data-center demand — further drops will be gradual.

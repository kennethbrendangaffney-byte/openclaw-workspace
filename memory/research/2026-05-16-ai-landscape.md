# AI Landscape Brief — 2026-05-16

## Key Findings

- **GPT-5.5 Instant is now the default ChatGPT model** (released May 5 by OpenAI), replacing earlier GPT-5 variants as the standard consumer-tier offering. GPT-5.5 Pro (top-tier reasoning) remains a separate paid upgrade.
- **Four Chinese AI labs dropped open-weights coding models within a 12-day window** in early May: Moonshot's Kimi K2.6, Z.ai's GLM-5.1, MiniMax M2.7, and a DeepSeek release — all targeting the coding/production use case and intensifying competition against Claude and GPT-5 series.
- **Benchmark leaderboard shows Gemini 3 Pro Preview at the top (~91%)**, narrowly ahead of GPT-5.2 xhigh (~84%), with the field compressed in the 65–73% range for mid-tier models. GPT-5 series leads overall but benchmark saturation at the top is pushing providers toward capability differentiation over raw score improvements.

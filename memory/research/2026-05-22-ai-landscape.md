# AI Landscape Brief — 2026-05-22

## New Model Releases
- **OpenAI** released **GPT-5.5 Instant** (May 5) as a new flagship fast tier, alongside **GPT-5.2** in xhigh and high variants — GPT-5.2 xhigh scores 84% on general benchmarks, narrowing the gap with Google's top spot.
- **Google's Gemini 3 family** continues to expand: **Gemini 3 Pro Preview** topped the general leaderboard at **91.0%**, with **Gemini 3 Flash** at 72.6%. March saw **Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite** launch as the cheapest-per-token option to date.
- **Anthropic** fielded **Claude Opus 4.6** and **Claude Sonnet 4.6**, with Opus 4.6 posting an **82.1% SWE-bench** coding score — the highest recorded for that benchmark. **xAI's Grok 4** also entered the coding leaderboard tied with Opus 4.6 at the top.

## Benchmark Results
- **Overall leaderboard** (LMCouncil, Mar 2026): Gemini 3 Pro Preview 91.0% → GPT-5.2 xhigh 84.0% → Gemini 3 Flash 72.6% → GPT-5.2 high 67.0% → GPT-5 high 66.0%.
- **Coding**: Claude Opus 4.6 and Grok 4 are co-leading; Gemini 3.1 Pro leads reasoning tasks.
- **Blind user test** (134 participants, 8 tasks): Claude won 4/8 rounds, ChatGPT won 1/8 — underscoring that leaderboard position doesn't always map to perceived quality.

## API & Pricing
- **Batch APIs** (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google) now ship at **50% off input token rates** with a 24-hour SLA — a significant cost lever for non-real-time workloads.
- **Price floor**: GPT-4.1 Nano sits at **$0.10/M tokens**, the cheapest major-model option. **Price ceiling**: premium tiers hit **$30/M tokens**.
- **Consumer plans**: OpenAI Pro tiers at $20–$200/mo; Anthropic Pro at ~$17/mo (annual) with a new $200/mo Max plan for heavy users. Google remains the most aggressive on per-token value in the Flash/Lite tier.

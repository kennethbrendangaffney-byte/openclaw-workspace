# AI Landscape Brief — 2026-05-27

## New Model Releases
- **GPT-5.5 Instant** (OpenAI, May 5) launched as the new mid-tier offering alongside GPT-5.5 Pro. DeepSeek V4, Claude Opus 4.7, and Gemma 4 also landed within a three-week window in April 2026, intensifying the frontier model race.
- Grok 4 leads SWE-bench raw coding scores at 75%, narrowly ahead of GPT-5.4 (74.9%) and Claude Opus 4.6 (74%+).

## Benchmark Results
- Every major frontier model now scores above 88% on MMLU — the benchmark has effectively plateaued as a discriminator. GPQA Diamond is now the leading reasoning benchmark, with Claude Mythos Preview leading at 94.6%.
- Standard benchmarks (coding, math) show 85–95% saturation across GPT-5.3, Claude Opus 4.6/4.7, Gemini 3 models; alternative evals are being developed to maintain discriminating signal.

## API & Pricing
- 109 out of 302 tracked models have had a price change in May 2026. Notable current tiers: Claude Opus 4.7 at $5/$25, GPT-5.5 at $5/$30, GPT-5.5 Pro at $30/$180, Gemini 3 Pro Deep Think at $4 per million tokens.
- API prices have dropped ~80% in the past 12 months, though Google's Gemini flash preview saw a significant cost increase as of mid-March 2026. Hybrid/credit-based pricing models are increasingly common.

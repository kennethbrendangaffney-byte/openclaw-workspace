## Findings

- **NousResearch Hermes Agent v0.14.0 drops May 16, 2026** — 808 commits, 633 merged PRs since last release. As of May 10 it's overtaken OpenClaw to claim **#1 on OpenRouter's global daily rankings** — the first time a local-runnable agent model has topped that chart. Hermes-4-14B (small dense variant for local inference) also released Aug 2025 — both pointing to NousResearch betting hard on local-first agentic AI.

- **llama.cpp vs Ollama performance gap widens** — community benchmarks consistently show llama.cpp running 1.5–1.8× faster than Ollama on the same hardware. NVFP4 (NVIDIA FP4 E2M1) quantization format support is in development for llama.cpp, which would let newer NVIDIA GPUs use sub-4-bit inference. llama.cpp weekly updates continue; a March 29, 2025 release (still the latest tagged) introduced notable performance improvements and the May 4 weekly report shows active development.

- **2026 is the year of the SLM on desktop** — Mistral's Ministral 3B and 8B were highlighted in a May 2026 survey as standout small models, alongside NousResearch's Hermes-4-14B. Consumer-grade local inference (6–8GB VRAM) is now the practical floor for running capable models, with Q4/Q5 GGUF quantization remaining the sweet spot for quality-vs-VRAM tradeoffs. An April 2026 guide notes Q5 is where models "appear capable of more complex tasks and are more attentive," with Q6 very close to Q8 full precision.

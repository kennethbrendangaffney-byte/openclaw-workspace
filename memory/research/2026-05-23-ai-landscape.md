# AI Landscape Brief — 2026-05-23

## Model Releases
- **GPT-5.5 Instant** (OpenAI, May 5) launched as the new ChatGPT default, optimized for speed and fewer hallucinations.
- **SubQ 1M-Preview** (Subquadratic, May 5) released as the first commercial subquadratic LLM with a 12M token context window.
- **Grok 4.3** (xAI, May 6) entered wider rollout following its April beta. **ZAYA1-8B** (Zyphra, May 6–7) shipped as an open-source (Apache 2.0) MoE model trained on AMD hardware.
- **Gemini 3.1 Flash Lite** (Google, May 8) launched as a lightweight, cost-optimised variant.

## Benchmark Highlights
- **LMCouncil benchmark (May 2026):** Gemini 3 Pro Preview leads at 91.0%, GPT-5.2 xhigh at 84.0%, Gemini 3 Flash at 72.6%.
- **SWE-bench Verified (coding):** Claude Sonnet 4.6 scores 82.1% vs Gemini 3's 63.8% — a significant coding advantage for Claude.
- **Grok 4 and Claude Opus 4.6** lead coding benchmarks; Gemini 3.1 Pro leads reasoning.

## API & Pricing Changes
- **Batch APIs** now ship at **50% input cost reduction** across OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google.
- **Cached-read discounts** increased to **95%** on leading providers.
- **DeepSeek V4-Pro** promo pricing: frontier-class output at **$0.87/1M tokens** — a 99.5% reduction vs GPT-5.5 Pro.
- **Open-weight hosted models** (Llama 4 405B, Qwen 3 235B, DeepSeek V4-Flash) converged to **$0.20–0.80/1M output tokens** across Together, Fireworks, Anyscale, and Groq.
- **Anthropic** added a 1-hour cache billing tier; **Google** launched a Gemini Flash batch tier.

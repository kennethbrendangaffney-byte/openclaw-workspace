# Passive Income & Automation Research — 2026-05-15

## KDP / Amazon Publishing
- **Still profitable, but changed.** Saturation is real — low-content journals and colouring books are crowded. A $160K case study shows it can still work but requires strategy, not volume.
- **Seasonal lever.** Q4 generates up to 30% of some publishers' yearly income — timing uploads to catch Christmas demand is a known tactic.
- **Direction shift.** Best-performing sellers are moving upmarket: fewer, higher-quality niche titles rather than bulk publishing.

## No-Code Automation Tools (2026)
- **Top 3:** Zapier (beginner-friendly), Make (visual workflows, more flexible), n8n (open-source, self-hostable, developer-favoured).
- **New entrants competing:** Gumloop, Clay, Apify, Arahi AI — AI-native workflow tools that go beyond traditional triggers-and-actions automation.
- **n8n momentum:** Continues gaining developer mindshare. Strong on self-hosting = full data control.
- **Zapier expanding AI:** Adding AI agent features, but at a price premium. Market is competitive — good for buyers.

## Side Hustles for Devs
- **High-leverage digital products:** Dev cheat sheets, API templates, Notion systems for niche industries.
- **Content as asset:** Technical blog or Substack with affiliate links + course upsell compounds well over time.
- **Staking edge:** Crypto staking (3–10% APY) gives devs an advantage — they understand the tech better than average.
- **SaaS / developer tools:** Small paid tools solving specific dev problems (CLI utilities, VS Code extensions) have low distribution friction.

## Bottom Line
KDP still viable but needs niche focus + seasonal timing. No-code space is healthy and competitive — good time to build automation workflows. Dev-specific side hustles lean toward digital products and tools rather than generic "side gig" advice.

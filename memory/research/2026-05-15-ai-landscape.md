# AI Landscape Brief — 2026-05-15

- **GPT-5.5 Instant** (OpenAI, May 5) launched as the new default ChatGPT model, with SubQ 1M-Preview also arriving in May.
- **Four Chinese labs dropped open-weights coding models in 12 days:** GLM-5.1 (Z.ai), MiniMax M2.7, Kimi K2.6 (Moonshot), and DeepSeek V4 — all targeting the coding benchmark leaderboard.
- **Gemini 3 Pro Deep Think** and **Gemini 3 Flash** entered the fray, slotting into a crowded frontier.

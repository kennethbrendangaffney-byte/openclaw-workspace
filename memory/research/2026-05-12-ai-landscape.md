## Full Summary

### Model Releases (May 2026)
May 2026 has been one of the heaviest release months in recent memory. Key releases include:

- **GPT-5.5** — OpenAI's latest in the GPT-5 family landed in April/May 2026, alongside a reported "GPT-5.5 Instant" variant optimised for speed.
- **DeepSeek V4 (full release)** — The full version of DeepSeek V4 shipped in May, following its preview. It's a major reasoning/performance upgrade over V3, now competing seriously with Claude and GPT-5 class models.
- **Gemma 4 31B** — Google's Gemma 4 31B parameter model arrived in May, positioned as a strong open-weight option for local deployment with quality approaching closed models.
- **Claude updates (4.7 / mid-series)** — Anthropic shipped a mid-cycle Claude update in late April/early May, with improvements to context handling and extended reasoning.
- **Grok 4.20** — xAI released Grok 4.20 with notable multimodal improvements.
- **Meta Muse Spark** — Meta is building a highly personalised AI assistant powered by its Muse Spark model, capable of autonomously performing tasks for users.
- **Qwen3-Coder-Next (80B)** — Released early 2026, reached performance close to top closed models while running locally — a notable milestone for open-weight coding models.

### Benchmark Landscape (May 2026)
- **Leaderboard convergence** — The model race has slowed in raw benchmark gains but the gaps are tightening. GPT-5 class, Claude 4.7, and DeepSeek V4 are effectively at parity across most standard benchmarks.
- **Benchmarks getting harder to trust** — Multiple sources note that benchmark scores are increasingly gamed or saturating, making real-world eval and production experience more important than leaderboard rankings.
- **96 out of 538 tracked models had a price change in May alone** — Benchmark rankings and pricing are now intertwined; models that underperform are being repriced downward aggressively.
- **Price/performance ratio is the new differentiator** — With API costs having dropped ~80% in the last 12 months, the competitive edge has shifted from "best model" to "best model at what price."
- **Local models closing the gap** — Qwen3-Coder-Next (80B) and Gemma 4 31B running locally now rival closed models on many tasks.

### API Pricing (2026)
- **General downward trend with exceptions** — API prices dropped ~80% in the last 12 months. Most providers have repriced downward as competition intensifies and inference costs fall.
- **Google Gemini price increase (March 2026)** — A notable counter-trend: Google increased costs for gemini-3-flash-preview in mid-March 2026, bucking the broader deflation.
- **Hybrid pricing models becoming standard** — 50+ AI companies have shifted toward credit-based or flat-rate hybrid structures alongside token billing.
- **Enterprise cost as competitive factor** — By 2026, Gartner forecasts AI services cost will become a chief competitive differentiator for enterprises. Businesses still on 2023-era pricing psychology are significantly overpaying.
- **Context window multipliers** — Token-based billing now includes context-length multipliers, making long-context models significantly more expensive than base rates suggest.
- **DeepSeek V3 / GPT-5 nano** remain the cheapest high-quality options (~$0.05–0.27/M tokens).

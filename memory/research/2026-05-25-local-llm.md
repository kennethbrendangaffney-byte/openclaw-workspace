# Local LLM & Edge AI Research — 2026-05-25

## Summary of Latest Developments

- **Ollama v0.30.0-rc15 (May 2026)** shipped a significant shift — it switched back to using llama.cpp directly under the hood, meaning GGUF models are once again first-class citizens. This closes the compatibility gap that frustrates local LLM users who rely on custom quantised files.

- **llama.cpp gained MCP client support** (tool-calling capability) and **parallel model loading across GPU contexts** in its March 2026 release. This brings llama.cpp closer to being a full agent runtime rather than just an inference engine.

- **Mistral's Ministral 3B and 8B** continue to be highlighted as state-of-the-art SLMs in 2026 surveys, while **Cohere's Tiny Aya (3B, Feb 2026)** positions itself as the "most capable multilingual open-weight model" at the 3B tier — notable for edge and mobile deployment.

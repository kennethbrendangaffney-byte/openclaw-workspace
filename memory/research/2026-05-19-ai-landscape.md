# AI Landscape Brief — 2026-05-19

## Model Releases
- **Google launches Gemini Diffusion (beta, May 2026)** — Google's first diffusion-based LLM, shifting from auto-regressive generation to a novel token-denoising approach. Available in preview for developers.
- **GPT-5.5 Instant** now the default ChatGPT model — a lighter, faster variant of GPT-5.5, positioned between GPT-4o and the full GPT-5.4 for everyday queries.
- **Claude Sonnet 4.6** widely available — Anthropic's mid-tier model continues to dominate coding tasks; Opus 4.5 (non-thinking mode) ranks #2 overall on the LM Council leaderboard behind GPT-5.2.

## Benchmarks
- **Claude Sonnet 4.6 scores 82.1% on SWE-bench Verified** vs Gemini 3's 63.8% — an 18-point lead on real GitHub issue resolution, reinforcing Claude's dominance in software engineering tasks.
- **GPT-5.2 tops current leaderboards at 27.4%** (LM Council), with Claude Opus 4.5 at 26.5% and Gemini 3 Pro Preview at 18.6% — GPT retains the edge; Gemini Flash scores notably lower at 9.8%.

## API & Pricing
- **Anthropic adds 1-hour prompt cache tier** (price-positive for sustained sessions) alongside its existing 5-minute tier.
- **Google launches Gemini Flash batch tier** — discounted asynchronous pricing for high-volume, non-latency-sensitive workloads.
- **Together AI cuts Llama 4 70B by 15%** — ongoing compression on open-weight models continues to drive down costs for self-hosted deployments.

# Local LLM & Edge AI Research — 2026-05-23

## 3-Bullet Summary

- **🔹 Gemma 4 drops (April 2026)** — Google released its most capable open model family to date on April 2, under Apache 2.0 (no more restrictive conditions). Includes dense and MoE variants purpose-built for advanced reasoning and agentic workflows. Available in Edge-to-workstation sizes. Major step for local inference.

- **🔹 Qwen3.5 small models + multimodal push** — Qwen released its small dense model series in March 2026 with native multimodal capability (visual QA, OCR, document understanding, basic video summarisation, spatial reasoning). Community热议 around a potential 0.6B variant for speculative decoding in larger Qwen models.

- **🔹 llama.cpp/Ollama split deepens** — Since ~August 2025, Ollama no longer links to llama.cpp as a library, instead using ggml directly. Migration to raw llama.cpp gaining popularity for throughput gains. GGUF Q4_K_M / Q5_K_M / Q6_K quantization schemes remain the standard for fitting 7B models into 8GB RAM.

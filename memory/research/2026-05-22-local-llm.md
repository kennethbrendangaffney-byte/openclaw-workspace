# Local LLM & Edge AI Research — 2026-05-22

## Summary
- **Small Models (1B–8B):** 2026 is the year of the SLM mainstream. Qwen3 (0.5B–7B) and Phi-4 dominate local/edge leaderboards; Llama 3.2 (1B/3B) runs on smartphones; Mistral-Nemo 12B fills the mid-range. The SLM category is now considered "good enough" for most prod use cases, with Q4_K_M quantization bringing 7B models down to ~3.5GB.
- **Ollama vs llama.cpp:** llama.cpp maintains a 1.5–1.8x raw throughput advantage over Ollama on same hardware, with community benchmarks confirming GPU sampling improvements (TopK, TopP, Temperature, minK, minP, MWA). Ollama still wins on UX/API simplicity; llama.cpp wins on control and speed. A May 2026 guide notes llama.cpp now has a CLI and Vulkan support competitive with Ollama.
- **Quantization:** No major new format breakthroughs this cycle, but Q4_K_M remains the practical sweet spot for local deployment (best quality-per-VRAM trade). FP16 → Q8 → Q5 → Q4 → Q2 forms a clear quality ladder, with Q4 at ~3.5GB for a 7B being the mainstream choice. A April 2026 guide recommends Q4_K_M as the default "don't think about it" level for most users.

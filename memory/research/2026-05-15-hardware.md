# Hardware Research — 2026-05-15

## GPU Prices

- **RTX 4080 Super** averaging ~$1,008 (current), relatively stable
- **RTX 4070 Ti Super** spotted as low as ~$739
- **Price surge since mid-Jan 2026:** RTX 5060 Ti, 5070 Ti, 5080, and RX 9070 XT all saw noticeable price increases
- Tom's Hardware GPU price index updated regularly — good benchmark to watch

## DDR5 RAM

- Significant volatility: a 32GB DDR5-6000 kit that cost ~$80 in mid-2025 reached ~$432 by early 2026 (>400% increase)
- Some recent signs of easing — prices may be stabilizing but still well above 2025 lows
- 32GB remains the sweet spot for most users; 64GB still pricey

## Single-Board Computers (SBC)

- **Orange Pi 5 Ultra** — compact, PCIe-enabled, dual-display, Wi-Fi included. Emerging as a strong Raspberry Pi alternative (Apr 2026)
- **Overall SBC market** showing contraction — Jeff Geerling ("this is no joke: the SBC hobby is dying") and others noting fewer new releases and softer hobbyist demand vs 2024
- Price comparison (Apr 2026) covers Raspberry Pi 5, Orange Pi 5 Ultra, Radxa ROCK 5B+, ODROID-M2

## Takeaway for Ken

GPU and RAM prices are elevated right now — not a great buying window unless urgent. SBC market offers better alternatives to Raspberry Pi at competitive prices. Worth holding off on GPU/RAM purchases until Q3-Q4 2026 if possible.

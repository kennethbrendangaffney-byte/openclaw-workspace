# AI Landscape Brief — 2026-05-21

## New Model Releases
- **GPT-5.5 Instant** (OpenAI, May 5) launched as the new default ChatGPT model, positioned as a fast, cost-efficient option. A **GPT-5.5 Pro** tier also appeared with higher capability ceiling.
- **Gemini 3 Flash** and **Gemini 3 Pro Preview** entered the arena in mid-May, with the Pro variant currently topping the benchmark charts.

## Benchmark Results
- **Gemini 3 Pro Preview** leads mainstream benchmarks at **91.0%**, followed by GPT-5.2 (xhigh) at 84.0% and GPT-5.2 (high) at 67.0%.
- **Claude Opus 4.7** and **Grok 4** continue to dominate coding-specific benchmarks, while **Gemini 3.1 Pro** retains the reasoning crown.

## API & Pricing
- **Claude Opus 4.7**: $5 / $25 per 1M tokens (input/output)
- **GPT-5.5**: $5 / $30; **GPT-5.5 Pro**: $30 / $180
- **Gemini 3 Pro Deep Think**: ~$4 / $18 — notably, **Google raised Gemini API costs** across the board effective mid-March 2026.
- Recall.ai also dropped meeting recording pricing to **$0.50/hr** (from $0.70) as of early May.
